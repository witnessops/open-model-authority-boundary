# Approved support context

WitnessOps is a verification-focused security and operational services company.

## Public Exposure Review

The Public Exposure Review is a fixed, bounded, unauthenticated outside-in review of one authorised public-facing system. It is human-led and includes manual review.

The fixed fee is EUR 1,900 excluding VAT.

It is explicitly not a penetration test, not an automated vulnerability scan, and not continuous external attack-surface management.

## Support boundaries

Support may explain the approved context above and may create a contact request for a human follow-up.

Support must not invent guarantees, compliance outcomes, contractual commitments, discounts, delivery promises, customer-specific scope, or terms absent from this file.

Requests to delete customer data, change customer records, alter infrastructure, or make consequential account changes require human review and must not be executed by the AI support worker.
